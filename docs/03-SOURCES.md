# Sources and the date each one was read

Every check points to the sources below by ID. Date read for all of them: 2026-11-24. Each source was opened and read on that date unless it is in the list at the bottom.

## How much to trust the wording

- Tier A: the text of the document itself was read directly (two sources: the ISO form CG 40 48 01 26 and one law firm article).
- Tier B: the page was opened and read through a page reader tool that summarizes. Facts and short phrases are as that tool reported them. Re-open the page before you rely on an exact quote.
- ISO forms are copyrighted by Insurance Services Office. This kit quotes only form numbers and short phrases, never a whole form.
- Trade press and law firm articles are secondary sources. They describe policies, they are not your policy. Your own policy wording always decides.
- Several of these pages sell services or products (insurance brokers, a law firm, an AI safety vendor named in one article). That is a reason to read them as views, not as facts about your policy.

## Sources that disagree

One page that was opened, Risk and Insurance (https://riskandinsurance.com/worried-about-ai-risk-double-check-your-gl-policies/, date not shown), says E&O, cyber and D&O policies have no AI exclusions. Two other opened sources (L1 and T8) and the Berkley reports (L1, W4) say the opposite for E&O and D&O. This kit does not use the Risk and Insurance page for any check. The honest reading is that practice differs by carrier and by year, which is why every check tells you to read your own policy.

## Numbers this kit leaves out on purpose

- Counts of AI exclusion filings or of carriers using them: the articles that gave counts either did not name who counted them or summarized a study this build could not check, so no count is used.
- A percentage rise in AI lawsuits from an insurer research team: a figure that comes from a vendor that sells AI insurance is not used.
- Any claim about how many insurers will adopt the ISO forms: one trade article quotes a forecast from a company that sells AI cover, and no source read for this kit gives a reliable count.
