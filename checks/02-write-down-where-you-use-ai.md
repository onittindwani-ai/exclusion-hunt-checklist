# Check 02: Write down everywhere your business uses AI, so you can analyze the risk

- Check number: 02
- Group: Start here (the 3 checks in the Reel)
- Policy types: Applies to all of your policies (it is the list you need before you can answer any of the other checks)

## Exact words to look for

Wording from the sources (a [common] tag means ordinary wording that proves nothing alone):

- `artificial intelligence`

Search aid words (helpers, not quotes from a form):

- `AI`
- `chatbot`
- `automated`

## Where it hides in a policy

Not a policy wording. It is your own list. It matters because insurers ask about AI use in applications and renewals, and because an exclusion can reach any use of AI, including AI that is built into software you already use.

## Why it matters

Brokers advise mapping where AI is used, including customer messages, contracts, financial information and professional advice, and who relies on AI output to decide something or serve a customer. Law firms say the same: write down how AI is used across the business, and whether the use is small and incidental or central to what you sell. You cannot ask a good question about cover until you know where AI touches your work.

## What you do

1. Make a list with one row per use: emails and marketing text, ads and images, a website chatbot, hiring or screening, bookkeeping help, contract drafting, customer support, notes and transcripts.
2. For each use write: the tool name, who uses it, who sees the output (staff only, or customers), and whether a person checks it first.
3. Include AI that is built into software you already pay for. It counts.

## Ask your broker or carrier

> Here is my list of where we use AI (attached). Which of these would you want me to disclose on my next application or renewal, and which policy would respond if one of them went wrong?

Send it by email so the answer is in writing.
