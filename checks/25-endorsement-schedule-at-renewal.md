# Check 25: Read the endorsement schedule at every renewal and log what changed

- Check number: 25
- Group: Contracts, applications and renewals
- Policy types: Every policy at renewal

## Exact words to look for

Wording from the sources (a [common] tag means ordinary wording that proves nothing alone):

- `CG 40 47`
- `CG 40 48`
- `CG 35 08`
- `artificial intelligence`

Search aid words (helpers, not quotes from a form):

- `forms and endorsements`
- `schedule of forms`
- `endorsement`
- `renewal`

## Where it hides in a policy

In the renewal quote or the new policy: the list of forms and endorsements. Compare it line by line with last year's list.

## Why it matters

One broker firm advises reading the endorsement schedule at every renewal because the forms are optional and one insurer may exclude AI while another does not. A law firm article says to check renewal proposals for new or expanded AI exclusions, since cover available today may not carry over. A trade source says clients renewing in the months after the ISO forms appeared are already in the window.

## What you do

1. Put this year's and last year's forms lists side by side.
2. Write every added or removed form in the renewal change log template.
3. Ask the broker about each addition before you accept the renewal.

## Ask your broker or carrier

> Please send me the forms and endorsements list for the renewal and mark every form that is new or changed since last year.

Send it by email so the answer is in writing.
