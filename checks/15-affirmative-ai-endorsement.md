# Check 15: Check any affirmative AI endorsement for which scenarios it actually lists

- Check number: 15
- Group: Management, employment and professional policies
- Policy types: Technology errors and omissions, cyber, professional liability

## Exact words to look for

Wording from the sources (a [common] tag means ordinary wording that proves nothing alone):

- `artificial intelligence`

Search aid words (helpers, not quotes from a form):

- `affirmative`
- `endorsement`
- `hallucination`
- `enumerated`
- `extends coverage`

## Where it hides in a policy

An affirmative endorsement is an add-on page that says the policy does cover certain AI losses. Look in the endorsement list for titles with AI in them, and read the list of covered events.

## Why it matters

Some insurers now add explicit AI cover. A law firm article warns that these endorsements may narrow cover to specific scenarios, so cover is only as wide as the list. Examples reported include a standalone product that names hallucinations (wrong AI output) and model failures. Read exactly what is listed and what is not.

## What you do

1. If you have an AI endorsement, list every event it names.
2. Compare that list with your AI list from check 2.
3. Ask your broker what is not on the list.

## Ask your broker or carrier

> Please send the list of events covered by the AI endorsement and tell me which of my AI uses fall outside that list.

Send it by email so the answer is in writing.
