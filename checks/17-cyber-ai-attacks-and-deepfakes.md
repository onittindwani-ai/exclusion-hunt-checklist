# Check 17: Check what your cyber policy says about AI driven attacks and deepfakes

- Check number: 17
- Group: Cyber, crime and property policies
- Policy types: Cyber

## Exact words to look for

Wording from the sources (a [common] tag means ordinary wording that proves nothing alone):

- `deepfake`
- `social engineering` [common]
- `artificial intelligence`

Search aid words (helpers, not quotes from a form):

- `impersonation`
- `AI driven`
- `phishing`
- `fraudulent`

## Where it hides in a policy

In the cyber policy's coverage list and endorsements. Some insurers add an endorsement that clarifies or expands cover for AI driven attacks, including deepfake schemes (fake voice, video or images of a real person).

## Why it matters

A law firm article reports that some carriers have offered cyber AI endorsements that clarify or expand cover for AI driven attacks, including deepfake schemes. Another law firm article describes cyber as currently the steadiest home for AI risk, but says cyber forms were not designed for every AI risk, in particular liability from AI outputs. Read what yours says.

## What you do

1. Search your cyber policy for deepfake, social engineering and artificial intelligence.
2. Note any endorsement that adds or clarifies AI related cover.
3. Ask your broker whether a staff member fooled by a fake voice or video would be covered.

## Ask your broker or carrier

> Does my cyber policy cover losses caused by AI driven attacks such as deepfake impersonation? Is there an endorsement, and what are its limits and conditions?

Send it by email so the answer is in writing.
