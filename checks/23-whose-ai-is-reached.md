# Check 23: Check whether the wording covers only your own AI or also AI from vendors and AI built into other software

- Check number: 23
- Group: Contracts, applications and renewals
- Policy types: Any policy with an AI exclusion

## Exact words to look for

Wording from the sources (a [common] tag means ordinary wording that proves nothing alone):

- `any actual or alleged use`

Search aid words (helpers, not quotes from a form):

- `third-party`
- `vendor`
- `incorporating`
- `by the insured`
- `products or services`

## Where it hides in a policy

In the sentence that says whose use of AI is excluded. Some wording speaks of use by the insured. Reported wording in another policy also reaches products or services that incorporate AI.

## Why it matters

Law firm articles report wording that reaches any use of generative AI by the insured, and another that reaches products or services that incorporate AI. One law firm lists carve outs for liability tied to third party AI tools. A law firm article also warns that an overbroad exclusion could reach the AI part of an ordinary email program. You may use AI without knowing it.

## What you do

1. Mark whether the sentence says use by you, use by anyone, or products that contain AI.
2. Add any software you use that includes an AI feature to your list.
3. Ask your broker to explain which of those are reached.

## Ask your broker or carrier

> Does the AI wording reach AI that a vendor built into software we use, or only AI we choose to use ourselves?

Send it by email so the answer is in writing.
