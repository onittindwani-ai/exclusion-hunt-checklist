# Check 08: Read the definition of artificial intelligence: narrow or very broad

- Check number: 08
- Group: Wording that decides how far an exclusion reaches
- Policy types: Any policy with an AI exclusion

## Exact words to look for

Wording from the sources (a [common] tag means ordinary wording that proves nothing alone):

- `machine-based learning system or model`
- `explicit or implicit objectives`
- `infers, from the input it receives`

Search aid words (helpers, not quotes from a form):

- `definition`
- `means`

## Where it hides in a policy

In the definitions section of the policy or inside the endorsement itself, usually a line that begins with the quoted term and the word means.

## Why it matters

The ISO definition reads that generative artificial intelligence is a machine-based learning system or model trained on data with the ability to create content or responses, including text, images, audio, video or code. A reported insurer definition is much wider: any machine-based system that, for explicit or implicit objectives, infers from the input it receives how to generate outputs. A wide definition can reach tools you have used for years. Law firms warn it is open to many readings.

## What you do

1. Find the definition in your AI related form. Copy it into the register.
2. Underline how many tools it could cover: does it say generative, or any machine based system?
3. Ask your broker to name three tools you use that it would and would not reach.

## Ask your broker or carrier

> Please send me the exact definition of artificial intelligence used in my policy, and tell me whether the tools on my AI list fall inside it.

Send it by email so the answer is in writing.
