# Check 24: Read the AI questions on your application and renewal forms before you answer

- Check number: 24
- Group: Contracts, applications and renewals
- Policy types: Every policy application and renewal

## Exact words to look for

Wording from the sources (a [common] tag means ordinary wording that proves nothing alone):

- `artificial intelligence`

Search aid words (helpers, not quotes from a form):

- `application`
- `questionnaire`
- `representation`
- `warranty`
- `do you use`
- `signed`

## Where it hides in a policy

On the application or renewal questionnaire you sign, often in a technology or operations section. Your answers can matter later in a coverage dispute.

## Why it matters

Law firms warn that answers about AI in applications may matter in later coverage disputes and may be out of date by the time you renew. They also say policyholders will likely see more questions about AI use. Answer from your AI list (check 2), keep a copy and, when your use changes, tell your broker.

## What you do

1. Do not guess. Use your AI list from check 2.
2. Keep a copy of every application as you signed it.
3. Tell your broker in writing when your AI use changes between renewals.

## Ask your broker or carrier

> Which AI related questions are on my application or renewal form, and how would you suggest I answer them accurately?

Send it by email so the answer is in writing.
