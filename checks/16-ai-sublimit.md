# Check 16: Look for an AI sublimit, a smaller cap inside the main limit

- Check number: 16
- Group: Management, employment and professional policies
- Policy types: Professional liability, cyber, D&O

## Exact words to look for

Wording from the sources (a [common] tag means ordinary wording that proves nothing alone):

- `sublimit` [common]

Search aid words (helpers, not quotes from a form):

- `sub-limit`
- `aggregate`
- `retention`
- `per claim`

## Where it hides in a policy

On the declarations page (the summary page at the front) and in endorsements. A sublimit is a smaller cap that applies to one kind of loss inside the main limit.

## Why it matters

A law firm article warns of a sublimit that is too small for the loss you could suffer, and says some insurers sub limit AI related claims instead of excluding them. A small cap can feel like cover until a real claim arrives. Also look at the retention, the part you pay first, because it may be larger than the cap.

## What you do

1. Find the declarations page. Write down the main limit, any sublimit and the retention.
2. Write what a mistake could cost you.
3. Compare the numbers. Ask your broker if the gap can be closed.

## Ask your broker or carrier

> Is there any sublimit, extra retention or separate aggregate for AI related claims on my policies? What are the amounts?

Send it by email so the answer is in writing.
