# Check 07: Look for an insurer's own AI exclusion that does not use the ISO form numbers

- Check number: 07
- Group: General liability forms from ISO
- Policy types: General liability and other policies

## Exact words to look for

Wording from the sources (a [common] tag means ordinary wording that proves nothing alone):

- `generative artificial intelligence`
- `any actual or alleged use`

Search aid words (helpers, not quotes from a form):

- `generative AI`
- `AI-generated`
- `created using`

## Where it hides in a policy

On the forms list under a company form number (not a CG number), or inside the base policy. Insurers can write their own wording, so searching for CG 40 47 alone will miss it.

## Why it matters

Trade press reports that several large insurers filed to adopt the ISO forms or their own equivalents. A law firm article reports examples such as an exclusion for content created using generative AI in the insured's services and an exclusion for any claim based on any actual or alleged use of generative AI by the insured. The wording can be broader or narrower than the ISO forms, so you must read it.

## What you do

1. Search the whole policy for the words generative artificial intelligence and artificial intelligence, not only for form numbers.
2. List every form that has a company number and read its title.
3. Ask your broker for any carrier specific AI wording in writing.

## Ask your broker or carrier

> Beyond the ISO form numbers, does my insurer use its own AI exclusion or AI definition anywhere in my policy? Please list the form number and title.

Send it by email so the answer is in writing.
