# Check 09: Watch for the reach words: arising out of, attributable to, in any way involving

- Check number: 09
- Group: Wording that decides how far an exclusion reaches
- Policy types: Any policy with an AI exclusion

## Exact words to look for

Wording from the sources (a [common] tag means ordinary wording that proves nothing alone):

- `arising out of` [common]
- `attributable to` [common]
- `based upon, arising out of, or attributable to`
- `in any way involving`

Search aid words (helpers, not quotes from a form):

- `in connection with`
- `related to`

## Where it hides in a policy

In the one sentence that states what is excluded. These are small connecting words, and they decide how far the exclusion reaches.

## Why it matters

A law firm article explains that the phrase arising out of is read broadly by courts to need only a causal connection, so even a claim with a small link to generative AI could fall inside. A trade source says the trigger is the connection to AI, not how much of the work AI did. Wording such as in any way involving, reported for one insurer, reaches further still. Policyholder lawyers advise asking to strike vague phrases like these or to add carve backs (exceptions) for small, incidental AI use.

## What you do

1. Find the sentence that says what is excluded. Copy it.
2. Circle the connecting words.
3. Ask your broker whether a narrower version or an exception for incidental AI use is possible.

## Ask your broker or carrier

> Can you ask the insurer to narrow the connecting words in the AI exclusion, or to add an exception for incidental AI use and for ordinary negligence claims?

Send it by email so the answer is in writing.
