# Check 27: Look up whether your insurer filed an AI exclusion in your state

- Check number: 27
- Group: Timing, filings and silence
- Policy types: Any policy; a public search anyone can use

## Exact words to look for

Wording from the sources (a [common] tag means ordinary wording that proves nothing alone):

- `CG 40 47`
- `CG 40 48`
- `CG 35 08`
- `artificial intelligence`

Search aid words (helpers, not quotes from a form):

- `SERFF`
- `filing`
- `form filing`

## Where it hides in a policy

Not in your policy. Each state keeps a public record of insurance forms that insurers file. It is searched through SERFF Filing Access, a service run by the National Association of Insurance Commissioners, usually reached from your state insurance department's website.

## Why it matters

Trade press reports that several large insurers have asked U.S. regulators for permission to exclude AI liabilities. One insurer told the press it was not seeking to use them and had no plans to implement them at this time, so a filing does not mean the form is used on your policy. You may not see every detail. A Washington state page says to submit a public records request if you cannot find a filing or cannot download a document.

## What you do

1. Open your own state insurance department website and look for SERFF Filing Access.
2. Search by your insurer's name and the form numbers above.
3. Do not treat a filing as proof your policy has the form. Ask your broker.

## Ask your broker or carrier

> Has my insurer filed an AI exclusion in my state, and do you expect it to be used on my policy at renewal?

Send it by email so the answer is in writing.
