# Check 03: Keep proof that a person checks what the AI sends out

- Check number: 03
- Group: Start here (the 3 checks in the Reel)
- Policy types: Applies to general liability, professional liability, cyber and management liability (it is evidence you can show an insurer)

## Exact words to look for

Wording from the sources (a [common] tag means ordinary wording that proves nothing alone):

- `artificial intelligence`

Search aid words (helpers, not quotes from a form):

- `human review`
- `oversight`
- `governance`
- `controls`
- `approval`

## Where it hides in a policy

Not a form number. Look for questions on your application or renewal questionnaire about how AI output is reviewed, approved or supervised. Your answer is the proof.

## Why it matters

One broker firm says clients who can show controls will be better placed to negotiate cover. A trade report on a RAND study says underwriting is moving toward questions about AI governance, such as human review of outputs. Separately, a law firm article notes that if a professional relies on unverified AI output with no supervision, an insurer may argue no professional service was provided. A short written record that a person checked AI work before it went out helps you in all of those situations. It does not guarantee cover.

## What you do

1. Pick one simple habit: a named person checks anything AI wrote before a customer or the public sees it.
2. Keep a log: date, what was checked, who checked it, what was changed. A shared spreadsheet is fine.
3. Keep the log with your insurance papers, and bring it to renewal.

## Ask your broker or carrier

> At renewal, what evidence of human review of AI output would your underwriters like to see from us, and in what format?

Send it by email so the answer is in writing.
