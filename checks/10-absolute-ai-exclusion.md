# Check 10: Look for an absolute AI exclusion on management liability, errors and omissions and fiduciary policies

- Check number: 10
- Group: Management, employment and professional policies
- Policy types: Directors and officers (D&O), errors and omissions (E&O), fiduciary liability

## Exact words to look for

Wording from the sources (a [common] tag means ordinary wording that proves nothing alone):

- `any actual or alleged use, deployment, or development of artificial intelligence`
- `based upon, arising out of, or attributable to`
- `PC 51380`

Search aid words (helpers, not quotes from a form):

- `absolute`
- `Artificial Intelligence Exclusion`

## Where it hides in a policy

As an endorsement on the forms list of a D&O, E&O or fiduciary policy. The word Absolute may appear in the title. Berkley's form is reported as PC 51380; other insurers will use other numbers.

## Why it matters

D&O covers company leaders for claims about how they ran the business. E&O covers mistakes in professional work. Fiduciary covers people who manage employee benefit plans. A law firm reports that one carrier introduced an absolute AI exclusion in these lines, barring claims based upon, arising out of or attributable to any actual or alleged use, deployment or development of artificial intelligence. Such an exclusion can reach claims where AI was only a small part.

## What you do

1. Search every D&O, E&O and fiduciary policy for the words artificial intelligence.
2. If you find an absolute exclusion, write down the exact title and number.
3. Ask your broker whether the same cover can be bought without it, and what it would cost.

## Ask your broker or carrier

> Do my management liability, errors and omissions or fiduciary policies carry an absolute or broad AI exclusion? If so, what is the form number, and can the insurer remove or narrow it at renewal?

Send it by email so the answer is in writing.
