# Check 05: Look for form CG 40 48, the general liability exclusion that covers Coverage B only

- Check number: 05
- Group: General liability forms from ISO
- Policy types: Commercial general liability

## Exact words to look for

Wording from the sources (a [common] tag means ordinary wording that proves nothing alone):

- `CG 40 48`
- `generative artificial intelligence`
- `personal and advertising injury` [common]
- `Coverage B` [common]

Search aid words (helpers, not quotes from a form):

- `Coverage B Only`

## Where it hides in a policy

Same places as check 4: the forms schedule or a separate add-on page. The title of the form includes the words Coverage B Only.

## Why it matters

This is the narrower version. The form text itself says the insurance does not apply to personal and advertising injury arising out of generative artificial intelligence. Personal and advertising injury covers things like claims that your advertising or published material harmed someone. If you use AI to write ads, posts or images, this is the form that matters most.

## What you do

1. Search the forms schedule for CG 40 48.
2. If it is there, list every way you use AI for ads, posts, images or website text (from check 2).
3. Ask your broker whether those uses are now outside Coverage B.

## Ask your broker or carrier

> Does my policy include CG 40 48 or any Coverage B exclusion for generative AI? We use AI to write marketing text and images. Would a claim about that content be covered?

Send it by email so the answer is in writing.
