# Check 14: Check how the policy defines professional services, and whether AI assisted work still counts

- Check number: 14
- Group: Management, employment and professional policies
- Policy types: Professional liability (errors and omissions)

## Exact words to look for

Wording from the sources (a [common] tag means ordinary wording that proves nothing alone):

- `professional service` [common]

Search aid words (helpers, not quotes from a form):

- `professional services`
- `services you provide`
- `rendering of`

## Where it hides in a policy

In the definitions section of an E&O policy, under the term professional services.

## Why it matters

A law firm article warns that, even where there is no AI exclusion, an insurer may argue that a professional who relies on unverified AI output, with no supervision or oversight, never rendered a professional service within the terms of the policy. The same article says an overbroad exclusion could even reach everyday tools such as an email program's AI feature. This is why check 3 (keeping proof of human review) matters.

## What you do

1. Copy the definition of professional services into the register.
2. Compare it with what you actually do for clients, and with your AI list from check 2.
3. Ask your broker whether work that used AI, and was reviewed by a person, still counts.

## Ask your broker or carrier

> Please confirm in writing that work I perform with AI tools and review personally is still a professional service under my policy.

Send it by email so the answer is in writing.
