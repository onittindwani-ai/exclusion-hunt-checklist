# Check 21: Check the cyber incident exclusion on your commercial property policy

- Check number: 21
- Group: Cyber, crime and property policies
- Policy types: Commercial property, businessowners

## Exact words to look for

Wording from the sources (a [common] tag means ordinary wording that proves nothing alone):

- `CP 10 75`
- `CP 10 76`
- `BP 15 60`
- `BP 15 61`
- `cyber incident`
- `Unauthorized access to or use of any computer system`

Search aid words (helpers, not quotes from a form):

- `Cyber Incident Exclusion`
- `computer system`

## Where it hides in a policy

On the forms list of a commercial property policy or businessowners policy. The 2020 analysis says ISO's program requires one of two cyber incident exclusions on every property policy it covers, with one narrow exception.

## Why it matters

Property insurance pays for physical loss or damage to your property. The exclusion removes loss caused by a cyber incident, such as unauthorized access to a computer system or harmful code, with some exceptions such as fire or explosion that results. RAND notes that AI risk spans several lines, including commercial property. This check is not an AI exclusion. It is a nearby one a business can be surprised by.

## What you do

1. Search the property forms list for the numbers above.
2. Read the definition of cyber incident and the exceptions.
3. Ask your broker whether a failure of a system you rely on could fall into it.

## Ask your broker or carrier

> Does my property policy have a cyber incident exclusion, and would a failure of an AI or automated system I rely on be treated as a cyber incident?

Send it by email so the answer is in writing.
