# Check 01: Ask your broker in writing if your policy has a generative AI exclusion, and if your cyber and professional policies cover AI mistakes

- Check number: 01
- Group: Start here (the 3 checks in the Reel)
- Policy types: General liability, cyber, professional liability (errors and omissions)

## Exact words to look for

Wording from the sources (a [common] tag means ordinary wording that proves nothing alone):

- `generative artificial intelligence`
- `artificial intelligence`
- `CG 40 47`
- `CG 40 48`
- `CG 35 08`

Search aid words (helpers, not quotes from a form):

- `generative AI`
- `AI exclusion`

## Where it hides in a policy

Not in one place. Ask for the list of every form and endorsement on each policy (often called the forms schedule or endorsement schedule), then read the titles. An exclusion for generative AI can sit on a general liability policy, while the question of whether your cyber or professional policy picks up the same mistake is answered somewhere else.

## Why it matters

Generative AI means AI that writes or makes things, like text, images or code. An exclusion is a part of the policy that says what it will not pay for. Some standard general liability add-on pages (endorsements) that remove cover for generative AI became available in January 2026, and each insurer decides whether to use them. Brokers say that if general liability excludes AI, you need to know whether your errors and omissions (professional liability) or cyber policy responds, or whether the mistake falls in a gap between policies.

## What you do

1. Write one short email to your broker with the question below. Send it by email so you have it in writing.
2. Attach or list every policy you hold: general liability, professional liability (if you have it), cyber, management liability, crime.
3. Keep the written answer. File it with the policy. Put the date in the exclusion register.

## Ask your broker or carrier

> Please tell me in writing, for each of my policies: (1) Is there any endorsement or wording that excludes or limits generative artificial intelligence, and what is its form number? (2) If general liability excludes it, does my cyber policy or my professional liability policy cover a mistake made with AI, or is there a gap? Please quote the policy wording that supports your answer.

Send it by email so the answer is in writing.
