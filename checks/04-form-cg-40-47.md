# Check 04: Look for form CG 40 47, the general liability exclusion that covers both Coverage A and Coverage B

- Check number: 04
- Group: General liability forms from ISO
- Policy types: Commercial general liability

## Exact words to look for

Wording from the sources (a [common] tag means ordinary wording that proves nothing alone):

- `CG 40 47`
- `generative artificial intelligence`
- `arising out of` [common]

Search aid words (helpers, not quotes from a form):

- `Exclusion Generative Artificial Intelligence`

## Where it hides in a policy

In the list of forms and endorsements at the front of a general liability policy, or as its own add-on page. The form number ends in an edition date. The January 2026 edition is written 01 26. An older or different edition, or a carrier's own version, may look different.

## Why it matters

Coverage A is the part of general liability that pays for injury to other people and damage to their property. Coverage B is the part that pays for personal and advertising injury, such as some claims about what you publish or advertise. Trade sources report that CG 40 47 removes both when the loss arises out of generative AI, and it is optional for each insurer. If it is on your policy, a claim connected to generative AI may be refused.

## What you do

1. Search the forms schedule for CG 40 47.
2. If you find it, write down the edition date and the page, and ask your broker what it changes for your actual work.
3. If you do not find it, ask in writing whether another form removes cover for AI (checks 7 to 9).

## Ask your broker or carrier

> Does my general liability policy include form CG 40 47 or any similar exclusion for generative artificial intelligence? If yes, please send the full form and explain in plain words what it removes for my business.

Send it by email so the answer is in writing.
