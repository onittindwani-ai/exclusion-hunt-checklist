# Check 06: Look for form CG 35 08, the exclusion for products and completed operations

- Check number: 06
- Group: General liability forms from ISO
- Policy types: Products and completed operations liability coverage part

## Exact words to look for

Wording from the sources (a [common] tag means ordinary wording that proves nothing alone):

- `CG 35 08`
- `generative artificial intelligence`
- `completed operations` [common]
- `bodily injury` [common]
- `property damage` [common]

Search aid words (helpers, not quotes from a form):

- `products`

## Where it hides in a policy

On policies with a separate products and completed operations coverage part, in the forms list or as an add-on page.

## Why it matters

Products and completed operations liability is cover for injury or damage caused by a product you made or sold, or by work you finished. Sources describe CG 35 08 as removing cover for bodily injury or property damage arising out of generative AI under that part. If you make or sell things, or finish work for customers, and AI helped design, check or document it, this is the form to find.

## What you do

1. Search the forms list for CG 35 08.
2. If your business makes products or finishes work, write down where AI is used (design, quality checks, instructions, safety paperwork).
3. Ask your broker how the exclusion would apply to those uses.

## Ask your broker or carrier

> Does my policy have CG 35 08 or a similar exclusion on products and completed operations for generative AI? If so, which of my AI uses does it reach?

Send it by email so the answer is in writing.
