# Check 28: If your policy is silent on AI, do not assume you are covered or not, and ask in writing

- Check number: 28
- Group: Timing, filings and silence
- Policy types: All policies

## Exact words to look for

Wording from the sources (a [common] tag means ordinary wording that proves nothing alone):

- `artificial intelligence`
- `generative artificial intelligence`

Search aid words (helpers, not quotes from a form):

- `AI`
- `machine learning`
- `automated`

## Where it hides in a policy

Silent AI means your policy neither excludes nor expressly covers AI. You find it by not finding any AI wording. A search that finds nothing proves nothing: text from a scanned PDF can be wrong.

## Why it matters

A law firm article says policyholders should not assume they have no cover for AI liabilities under existing policies, and that courts generally read exclusions narrowly. A RAND study, as reported, says most carriers stay silent and that silence leaves cover untested and open to dispute. A law firm gives examples of older cover that may respond, such as general liability for injury from an AI powered machine and D&O for a misleading statement. The only safe step is to ask your insurer in writing.

## What you do

1. Run the scan tool on each policy. If it finds no AI wording, write silent in the register.
2. Ask the broker to get the carrier's written position on your top three AI uses.
3. Never rely on a phone call. Get it in writing.

## Ask your broker or carrier

> My policy has no wording about AI. Please ask the insurer to confirm in writing whether it would treat claims connected to my listed AI uses as covered, excluded or uncertain.

Send it by email so the answer is in writing.
