# Check 13: Check whether your professional liability policy excludes or caps AI mistakes

- Check number: 13
- Group: Management, employment and professional policies
- Policy types: Professional liability (errors and omissions)

## Exact words to look for

Wording from the sources (a [common] tag means ordinary wording that proves nothing alone):

- `artificial intelligence`
- `generative artificial intelligence`

Search aid words (helpers, not quotes from a form):

- `errors and omissions`
- `professional services`
- `sublimit`
- `sub-limit`

## Where it hides in a policy

In the exclusions and endorsements of your errors and omissions (E&O) policy. E&O pays when a client says your professional work was wrong. Some insurers add an AI exclusion or a smaller cap just for AI related claims.

## Why it matters

A law firm article reports that E&O insurers are scrutinizing AI assisted professional services, with some excluding or sub limiting AI related claims, and gives the example of a law firm's malpractice policy and AI invented citations. Other sources also say exclusions are spreading across lines. If you advise, design, write for or decide things for clients, this is a key policy to check.

## What you do

1. Search your E&O policy for artificial intelligence, generative and AI.
2. Write down any cap or exclusion.
3. Ask your broker to confirm in writing whether AI assisted work is covered like other work.

## Ask your broker or carrier

> Does my professional liability policy exclude, cap or limit claims where AI helped produce the work? Please give the form and the wording.

Send it by email so the answer is in writing.
