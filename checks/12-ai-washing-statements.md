# Check 12: Check cover for claims about what you said about your AI (AI washing)

- Check number: 12
- Group: Management, employment and professional policies
- Policy types: Directors and officers (D&O)

## Exact words to look for

Wording from the sources (a [common] tag means ordinary wording that proves nothing alone):

- `any actual or alleged use, deployment, or development of artificial intelligence`

Search aid words (helpers, not quotes from a form):

- `statements`
- `misrepresentation`
- `securities claim`
- `regulatory`

## Where it hides in a policy

In the D&O exclusions and in the AI exclusion if there is one. Law firms report that some AI exclusions also reach statements about AI, such as business plans and AI risk assessments.

## Why it matters

AI washing is the term the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission used in March 2024 when it fined two investment advisers a total of 400,000 dollars for overstating how they used AI. Claims over statements about AI can reach management liability policies. Reported absolute exclusions have a part aimed at statements about AI. If you describe your AI abilities in marketing or to investors, you want to know whether that is covered.

## What you do

1. Collect every public claim you make about AI: website, ads, proposals, investor decks.
2. Check each claim is true and provable.
3. Ask your broker whether claims about those statements would be covered by your management liability policy.

## Ask your broker or carrier

> If someone says our statements about our AI abilities were misleading, does my D&O policy respond, or does an AI exclusion remove cover?

Send it by email so the answer is in writing.
