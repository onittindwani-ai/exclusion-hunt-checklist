# Check 11: Check employment practices cover for AI used in hiring and people decisions

- Check number: 11
- Group: Management, employment and professional policies
- Policy types: Employment practices liability (EPLI), D&O

## Exact words to look for

Wording from the sources (a [common] tag means ordinary wording that proves nothing alone):

- `employment practices` [common]
- `arising out of any use, development, or deployment of artificial intelligence`
- `any actual or alleged use, deployment, or development of artificial intelligence`

Search aid words (helpers, not quotes from a form):

- `hiring`
- `screening`
- `discrimination`
- `applicant`

## Where it hides in a policy

In the EPLI policy exclusions or its forms list, and in the D&O policy. Look for AI wording next to the words employment, hiring or discrimination.

## Why it matters

Employment practices liability (EPLI) covers claims by employees and job applicants, such as unfair treatment. Sources report that AI exclusions are appearing in employment practices and management liability policies, and a law firm lists discrimination claims from AI hiring tools as an area of concern. Lawsuits alleging bias in AI hiring have been filed, and a trade article says an AI resume screening claim could fall under such an exclusion.

## What you do

1. If you use any AI to screen, rank or write to job applicants or staff, note it in your AI list.
2. Search your EPLI policy for the words artificial intelligence.
3. Ask your broker whether an AI hiring tool claim is covered, excluded or unclear.

## Ask your broker or carrier

> Does my employment practices policy exclude or limit claims connected to AI used in hiring, screening or employee decisions? Please quote the wording.

Send it by email so the answer is in writing.
