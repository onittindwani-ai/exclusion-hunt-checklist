# Broker question list

Use this for a call or a meeting, then send the answers back to your broker by email and ask them to confirm in writing. The rows marked SAMPLE show the style. Replace them with your own. This is not insurance or legal advice, and the answers apply only to your own policies.

## The three questions from the Reel (checks 1 to 3)

1. Does any policy of mine have a generative AI exclusion, and does my cyber policy or my professional liability policy cover a mistake made with AI? Please answer in writing.
2. Here is my list of where we use AI (use `ai-use-map.csv`). Which uses would you disclose on the next application?
3. What proof that a person checks AI output would help at renewal?

## General liability (checks 4 to 9)

4. Is form CG 40 47, CG 40 48 or CG 35 08 on my policy, or does the insurer use its own AI wording? Which edition?
5. What does the definition of AI say? Does it cover only generative AI?
6. Can the connecting words (arising out of, in any way involving) be narrowed, or an exception added for small, incidental AI use?

## Professional, management and employment policies (checks 10 to 16)

7. Is there an absolute AI exclusion on D&O, E&O or fiduciary cover?
8. Is AI used in hiring covered under employment practices cover?
9. Is there a sublimit, a separate aggregate or a bigger retention for AI claims?

## Cyber, crime and property (checks 17 to 21)

10. Does cyber cover respond if AI sends wrong information and nobody has hacked us?
11. What is the cap and what are the conditions of our social engineering cover? Is a fake voice call included?
12. Could a failure of a system we rely on be a cyber incident under the property exclusion?

## Contracts, applications, renewals and timing (checks 22 to 28)

13. Do the promises in our customer contracts and our AI vendor terms fit inside our cover?
14. Which questions about AI are on the application, and how should we answer them?
15. Please mark every form that is new since last year on the renewal forms list.
16. What are the retroactive date and extended reporting terms, and when must we report a possible problem?
17. Has the insurer filed an AI exclusion in our state, and will it be used on our policy?
18. If the policy is silent on AI, will the insurer confirm in writing how it would treat our listed AI uses?
