# Broker email draft prompt

Paste this after a policy hunt. The assistant writes an email you can edit and send yourself. It never sends anything.

```text
Write a short, polite email from me to my insurance broker, using the results of the policy hunt above.

Rules:
- Ask in writing. Ask the broker to answer in writing and to quote the policy wording that supports each answer.
- Use at most 6 questions. Put the three questions from the Reel first: (1) is there a generative AI exclusion, and do my cyber and professional policies cover AI mistakes, (2) which of my AI uses should I disclose, (3) what proof of human review of AI output would help at renewal.
- Then add the questions that match the checks that were FOUND or UNCLEAR. Use the wording from the "Ask your broker or carrier" part of those checks.
- Do not say what my policy covers. Do not accuse anyone. Do not include personal data I did not give you. Use [YOUR NAME] and [POLICY NUMBER] as blanks for me to fill.
- Plain friendly words, no dashes.
- End by asking for a reply within 10 working days.
```
