# Report prompt

Paste this at the end to get a one page summary and rows for the register.

```text
Make a one page report of my AI exclusion hunt, from the results above.

Sections:
1. What was checked: policy names, carriers, how many of the 28 checks were run, how many were FOUND, UNCLEAR, NOT FOUND, and how many still need me.
2. The wording that was FOUND: the exact line, the page, the form, one plain sentence of what it says.
3. What is unclear and why (scanned text, missing forms schedule, policy not provided).
4. The five questions to ask my broker, in order.
5. Rows for `templates/exclusion-register.csv`, as CSV, one per check that was FOUND or UNCLEAR.

Rules: not insurance or legal advice, never say I am covered or not covered, never guess, say "not seen in this text" instead of "does not exist". Plain words, no dashes.
```
