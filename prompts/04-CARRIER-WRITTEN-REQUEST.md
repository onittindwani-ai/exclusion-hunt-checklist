# Carrier written request prompt

Use this if your broker cannot answer, or you want the insurer's own position. Many people send it through the broker. It is a request for information, not a claim.

```text
Draft a short written request from me to my insurer (to be sent through my broker if I have one). I want the insurer's written position on how it treats AI.

Include:
- The policy name and number as blanks: [POLICY NAME], [POLICY NUMBER], [YOUR NAME].
- A numbered list of my AI uses (I will paste them).
- These questions: Does any form on my policy exclude or limit generative artificial intelligence or artificial intelligence? Please list the form numbers and send the full forms. How is artificial intelligence defined? Is there a sublimit for AI related claims? Has the insurer filed AI exclusions in my state and does it plan to use them on my policy at renewal? If my policy is silent on AI, please confirm in writing how the insurer would treat claims connected to my listed AI uses.
- A line that says this is a request for information and not a notice of claim. If I have a real problem to report, I will tell my broker separately in writing.

Rules: polite, plain words, no dashes, no legal threats, no statements about what is covered.
```

Note: if you have already had an AI related loss or someone has complained, tell your broker right away in writing and ask how to give notice. Do not wait for the answer to this request.
